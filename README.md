# KeeperHQ

A web-based, platform-agnostic hub for fantasy keeper leagues. KeeperHQ
replaces the commissioner's spreadsheet plus group chat with a single
shared link your league uses to view rosters, see keeper costs, and
submit selections.

## Why

Most fantasy platforms either don't support keeper leagues or only
support a narrow set of keeper rules. Commissioners running leagues
that fall outside those rules — or running leagues across multiple
platforms — end up doing the keeper work by hand. KeeperHQ is the
shared workspace for that work, so the commissioner stops being the
bottleneck and league members can self-serve.

## Supports

- **Any fantasy platform** — Yahoo, ESPN, Sleeper, and others. Paste
  in rosters and draft results from your platform's exports;
  KeeperHQ doesn't care where they came from.
- **Any sport** — NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL.
- **Both keeper formats** — snake-draft leagues with multi-year
  contracts, and auction leagues with cost-keep rules.

## Who it's for

Keeper-league commissioners, and the league members they share the
link with. Commissioners configure the rules and import the data;
members view their roster, see eligible keepers and costs, and
submit their selections.

## Status

Alpha. Local-first — your league data lives in your browser.
