# KeeperHQ

A web-based, platform-agnostic tool for fantasy keeper-league
commissioners. KeeperHQ replaces the spreadsheet you've been
maintaining by hand to track rosters, keeper costs, contracts,
and payouts.

## Why

Most fantasy platforms either don't support keeper leagues or
only support a narrow set of keeper rules. Commissioners running
leagues that fall outside those rules — or running leagues across
multiple platforms — end up doing the keeper work in spreadsheets.
KeeperHQ is a purpose-built workspace for that work.

## Supports

- **Any fantasy platform** — Yahoo, ESPN, Sleeper, and others.
  Paste in rosters and draft results from your platform's exports;
  KeeperHQ doesn't care where they came from.
- **Any sport** — NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL.
- **Both keeper formats** — snake-draft leagues with multi-year
  contracts, and auction leagues with cost-keep rules.

## Status

Alpha, and currently a **single-user, local-first tool** — your
league data lives in your own browser. That means it's only
available on the device you set it up on, and isn't yet shareable
with other people. Multi-device and multi-user (commissioner +
league members) are on the roadmap.
